# One-snapshot reads and acknowledgement

Read this before touching a read whose answer depends on an authority that can change under it:
an exact-date grant, a link's scope, a church switch, a group filter or a role. Background: M8.1.

## The rule

- A surface authorized that way reads its authority and what it shows in one snapshot, so it never
  pairs an old grant with a new date or the other way round. The surfaces it covers are listed in
  the first row below; a new surface of that kind joins them, with a test.
- A participating reader's or the guarded edit's answer is acknowledged (`acknowledgeDecision`)
  only for the viewer its deciding snapshot judged or its writer locked (`sessionJudgment`), once
  the transaction has ended, on an answer or a refusal it decided. Never on a 401, a failure, a
  failed COMMIT or an unconfirmed rollback (a reader's runs in `decidingSnapshot`), and a missing
  resource answers exactly like a hidden one. `/auth/bootstrap` acknowledges the middleware's
  fresh read instead.
- Pushes, guardian copies and batch email follow the same rule; their details are in `push.md`
  and `mail.md`.
